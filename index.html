Digital Court Management System — Prototype Guide

Welcome to the Digital Court System Prototype! This application simulates a full-featured e-Judiciary and court management workflow without requiring an external database (using local browser memory & persistence).

Key Features Implemented:

  • Role Simulation: Switch between Court Registrar (Admin), Presiding Judge, and Senior Counsel from the top right profile.
  • Master Case Docket: Search, filter by category/judge/priority/status, view detailed Dossier, update status, and export to CSV.
  • e-Filing Portal: Complete multi-part electronic case registration with live receipt generation and document vault.
  • Daily Cause List: Courtroom schedules, active hearings, filter by date, and kiosk display mode.
  • Virtual Courtroom: Simulated video hearing with animated audio waveforms, live speech-to-text transcriptions, and judicial gavel sound/animation.
  • Orders & Judgments: Searchable repository of court decisions with printable official order sheets featuring High Court seal and digital signatures.
  • e-Summons Generator: Instant digital summons generator with official judicial notice layout.
  • Precedents Library: Landmark citations and legal ratios quick lookup.
